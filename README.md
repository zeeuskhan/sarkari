# Rajasthan News 24

Hindi-first Rajasthan and India news portal frontend.

## Features
- Responsive Hindi news homepage
- Category-based Google News RSS search via rss2json
- SEO metadata, robots.txt and sitemap.xml
- About and privacy pages

## Deploy
Connected to Vercel. Pushes to `main` trigger production deployment.

## Before public launch
- Replace contact@example.com with a real editorial email.
- Verify the Google News RSS / rss2json feed availability and terms.
- Add an editorial workflow, original reporting and fact-checking.
- Replace the sitemap/canonical URL if the Vercel project slug changes.
- Configure real newsletter handling before collecting email addresses.

News headlines link to their original publishers. Do not present unverified content as original reporting.
