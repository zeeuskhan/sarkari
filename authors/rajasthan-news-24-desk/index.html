Rajasthan News 24 Desk is the collective editorial byline used for articles prepared for this publication. It represents the publication's editorial process, not a claim that every article was written by one named individual.

What the desk does

The desk researches developments using available reporting, official notices and primary sources where possible. It prepares original summaries, adds relevant background, checks names and figures against cited material, and updates articles when reliable new information becomes available.

How to read our articles

Each article should identify its publication date, relevant source attribution and any important uncertainty. Allegations are described as allegations, and developing reports may change as authorities release further information. Source links are provided so readers can verify important claims.
