We welcome factual feedback, correction requests, source suggestions and copyright or image-credit concerns about material published on this website.

What to include in a correction request

  • The full URL of the article.
  • The exact sentence, figure or image you are contacting us about.
  • A brief explanation and a reliable source that supports the requested change.
