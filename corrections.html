Corrections Policy

Advertisement

Rajasthan News 24 aims to publish accurate, clear and fairly attributed information. If a published article contains a factual error, we review the concern against reliable evidence and correct the page when warranted.

How to report a possible error

When reporting a concern, identify the article URL, quote the specific sentence or figure, explain what is incorrect, and provide a reliable supporting source such as an official notice, public record or direct statement. Please distinguish factual errors from differences of opinion.

How we handle corrections

  • We review the claim and supporting evidence before changing an article.
  • Material corrections are made clearly and promptly after verification.
  • When a significant factual change is made, the article should explain what was corrected and when.
  • We do not change publication dates simply to make an article appear newer.
  • Minor spelling or formatting fixes may be made without a correction note if they do not change the meaning.

Updates and developing stories

For developing events, new confirmed information may be added as it becomes available. An update is not a correction unless an earlier statement was inaccurate. We aim to make that distinction clear to readers.

Contact

Our public newsroom contact details are being finalised. Please do not send sensitive personal information. A correction request should include only the information needed to identify and verify the issue.
