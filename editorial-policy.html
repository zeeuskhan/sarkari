Editorial Policy

Rajasthan News 24 aims to publish useful, clear and reliable news in original language. We do not copy and paste complete articles, paragraphs or headlines from other publishers and present them as our own work.

Original writing and human tone

  • Stories are written independently using verified facts, context and editorial judgment. Merely changing words in another publisher's article is not original reporting.
  • We use clear language, avoid misleading headlines and do not stuff keywords.
  • Where useful, we explain local context, background and the possible public impact of a development.
  • AI tools may assist research or editing, but factual checks and final editorial responsibility remain with the publisher.

Sources and fact-checking

  • Important claims are checked against official documents, relevant authorities and more than one reliable source wherever possible.
  • Information drawn from other publishers is attributed. Attribution does not mean we claim their reporting as our own.
  • Allegations, investigations and unverified claims are not presented as established facts. Responses from relevant parties are included when available.
  • Uncertainty and conflicting figures are disclosed rather than concealed.

Images and thumbnails

  • An image being visible on another website does not make it free to reuse.
  • We use a source publisher's image only when permission, a suitable licence or another valid legal basis allows it. Required photo credits are provided.
  • When reuse is not authorised, we use licensed imagery or original illustrations created for the story.
  • Images must be relevant and must not misrepresent an event. AI-generated illustrations are not presented as real photographs.

Corrections and updates

When an error is identified, we correct it using reliable evidence. Significant corrections should include an update date and a clear explanation of what changed.

On-site reading and source attribution

Our original articles are published on this website so readers can read the story here. We credit the publishers and official sources used in our research. We do not reproduce their complete copyrighted articles. RSS headline summaries, where displayed, are clearly identified as summaries and are not represented as full original reporting.

Contact

For factual corrections, image credits or copyright concerns, use the active contact channel listed on this website.

Authorship

Articles using the collective byline are attributed to the Rajasthan News 24 Desk author profile. We do not invent individual reporter names, credentials or experience.

Corrections

Read our Corrections Policy for how to submit evidence and how material corrections are handled. Our public contact details are being finalised and are not replaced with a fabricated email address.
