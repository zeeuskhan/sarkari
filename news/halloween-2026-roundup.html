Halloween 2026: 30 News Stories, Events and Explainers

How to use this special coverage collection

The stories in this collection serve different purposes. Event reports describe a particular gathering or public development and should be read with their linked source material. Culture and history explainers provide background rather than breaking news. Safety features offer general precautions and do not replace advice from local authorities, venue operators or emergency services. Where a report concerns an ongoing police investigation, early information can change as officials release verified updates.

Halloween in India: a changing urban celebration

Halloween is not a traditional national festival in India, but themed events have become visible in some cities through restaurants, shopping centres, schools, entertainment venues and private gatherings. The format differs by location. Some events focus on costumes and family activities, while others use horror films, live music, themed food or nightlife programming. Organisers should communicate age restrictions, ticket terms, venue rules and safety arrangements clearly.

Practical safety for families and event organisers

For children, choose costumes that allow clear vision and movement, use visible or reflective details after dark and accompany younger participants near roads. Check that masks do not obstruct breathing and that costume accessories are not sharp. At public events, identify exits, meeting points and staff assistance before crowds build. People attending late-night events should plan transport in advance and avoid leaving drinks unattended.

Respectful celebrations and local rules

Costumes and decorations should be safe and respectful of the community. Avoid blocking public roads, damaging property, using open flames near decorations or frightening people who have not chosen to participate. Noise limits, venue capacity, fire-safety requirements and local police directions remain applicable even when an event is marketed as a festival or private party.
