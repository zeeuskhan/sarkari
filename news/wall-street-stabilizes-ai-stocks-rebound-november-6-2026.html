Wall Street Stabilizes as AI Stocks Rebound After Two-Day Sell-Off

Published November 6, 2026 · 2:04 p.m. ET · Rajasthan News 24 US Desk

NEW YORK, November 6, 2026: U.S. stocks steadied Friday as technology shares recovered from a steep two-day decline. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq were higher in afternoon trading, but the Dow was nearly flat and the week remained negative.

U.S. stocks found some footing Friday after a sharp technology-led sell-off earlier in the week. At about 1:50 p.m. Eastern time, the S&P 500 was up 0.7%, the Nasdaq Composite had gained 1.4%, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average was down 6 points, less than 0.1%, according to the Associated Press. These are intraday readings, not final closing prices.

Chip shares led the recovery

Nvidia rose 3.2% after losing ground in the previous two sessions. AI infrastructure company IREN jumped 18.7% after reporting stronger quarterly results and announcing two new contracts. Semiconductor-equipment maker Lam Research gained 7.8% after announcing a program to return up to $25 billion to investors through share buybacks.

Not every company recovered. Axon Enterprise fell 16.6% after reporting weaker quarterly results than analysts expected and announcing a $1.2 billion investment in drone maker Skydio. Charter Communications dropped 5.5% after reporting a quarterly loss.

Why AI stocks remain volatile

Investors are questioning whether the cost of building AI data centers, buying chips and securing electricity will translate into profits quickly enough. High borrowing costs and expensive valuations were already concerns. Election results added uncertainty about possible oversight and local restrictions on new projects.

Treasury yields and the Fed

The 10-year Treasury yield edged up to 4.85% from 4.84% late Thursday in the AP’s afternoon report. A stronger jobs report also led traders to assign a higher probability to a possible Fed rate increase in December. Higher rates can make borrowing more expensive and weigh on valuations for companies whose expected profits are far in the future.

Investors will be watching inflation data, Fed comments, company earnings and evidence about AI spending. One positive trading session does not erase the week’s losses or resolve the questions that triggered the sell-off.
