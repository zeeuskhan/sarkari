Russell Brand Found Guilty in UK: Verdict, Sentencing and Why the Case Is Trending in the US

Russell Brand was convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault at Southwark Crown Court. Here is what the jury decided, the charges he beat and what happens next.

Published November 6, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 US Desk · Updated as of publication

Russell Brand, the British comedian, actor and former broadcaster, is at the center of a major court story after a jury at London's Southwark Crown Court found him guilty of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault. The verdicts were delivered on Thursday, November 5, after a four-week trial. Brand is due to be sentenced on Monday, November 9, according to the Crown Prosecution Service.

What did the jury decide?

The jury convicted Brand on four counts involving four women. The offences the jury found proved took place between 2004 and 2009. The Crown Prosecution Service said the convictions were for one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault.

The jury did not find Brand guilty of every charge. He was acquitted of two other counts of rape and one count of attempted indecent assault. Those acquittals are an important part of the outcome and should not be omitted when describing the case.

What happens next in the UK court case?

Brand was remanded in custody after the verdicts. The judge indicated that he faces a significant custodial sentence, and sentencing is scheduled for November 9. The sentence had not been handed down at the time this article was published. The final punishment and any subsequent legal steps should therefore not be presented as settled facts yet.

Police and prosecutors have also referred to other complaints that are being examined separately. Those matters are not part of the four convictions announced on November 5. An allegation or investigation is not a conviction, and any further charges would have to go through the legal process.

What was Brand's position during the trial?

Brand denied the charges and pleaded not guilty. During the proceedings, the defence position was that the sexual encounters were consensual. The jury considered the evidence presented in court and returned the verdicts described above. This article reports the jury's decisions and does not independently determine disputed events beyond those verdicts.

Who is Russell Brand?

Brand is a British comedian, actor, writer and former television and radio presenter who became widely known in the UK and later appeared in films and US entertainment projects. In recent years he has also built a large online audience through commentary and interviews. His career and public profile help explain why a UK criminal case can draw substantial attention from readers in the United States.

What US readers should understand about the case

This is a British criminal case, not a prosecution in the United States. The trial took place at Southwark Crown Court in London, and the verdict was delivered under the criminal justice system of England and Wales. US readers should look to the court and UK prosecuting authorities for case updates rather than assuming US criminal procedure applies.

For readers following the story, the key confirmed points are the four guilty verdicts, the three acquittals, Brand's denial of the charges and the scheduled sentencing date.
