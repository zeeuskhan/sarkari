e-Shram Card 2026: Eligibility, Registration, UAN and How Unorganised Workers Can Apply

Understand who can register on e-Shram, what the Universal Account Number means, how to use official registration channels and how to avoid fraud.

Published November 2, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 Desk

New Delhi, November 2, 2026: India's e-Shram portal maintains a national database of unorganised workers, including construction workers, migrant workers, street vendors, domestic workers, agricultural workers and gig or platform workers. The system is intended to help improve identification and delivery of social-security services to workers who may not be covered by formal employment records.

Who can register?

The National Informatics Centre's e-Shram project page says unorganised-sector workers aged 16 to 59 are eligible to register. It lists groups such as construction workers, migrant workers, gig and platform workers, street vendors, domestic workers and agricultural workers. Eligibility and exclusions should always be checked on the official portal before registration.

What is the UAN?

After registration, a worker is assigned a Universal Account Number (UAN). The NIC description says the UAN card can be downloaded after registration. The UAN is an identifying number for the e-Shram database. It should not be mistaken for an automatic approval for every government benefit or a guarantee of a fixed monthly payment.

How to register through official channels

  1. Open the official e-Shram portal.
  2. Use the self-registration facility if you can complete the required online verification.
  3. If you need help, use an authorised Common Service Centre (CSC) or an applicable state service centre.
  4. Keep your mobile phone available for verification and provide information only on the official portal or to an authorised operator.
  5. After successful registration, save your UAN and download the card if the service makes it available.

What information may be requested?

The NIC project description lists Aadhaar KYC, mobile-number verification, bank-account status and details such as education, occupation and skills among the data handled in the registration workflow. Keep the information accurate. If a detail is wrong, use the official correction or assistance channel rather than creating duplicate registrations.

Does an e-Shram card guarantee financial assistance?

No. Registration creates a record in the national database. Access to a particular welfare scheme depends on that scheme's separate eligibility conditions, application process and current government rules. Be cautious of messages claiming that every e-Shram cardholder is entitled to a guaranteed monthly cash payment or that a fee is required to unlock a benefit.

Where can workers get help?

The NIC e-Shram page lists a toll-free helpdesk number, 14434, and says assistance is available in multiple languages from Monday to Saturday. Check the current contact information on the official e-Shram website before calling, as helpdesk arrangements can change.

Fraud-prevention checklist

  • Use the official eshram.gov.in domain or a recognised service centre.
  • Never share your bank PIN, card CVV or UPI PIN with an operator.
  • Do not pay someone who promises guaranteed benefits or priority approval.
  • Keep your UAN private and use official channels for corrections.

Conclusion

e-Shram is a national worker database designed to support access to social-security programmes. Workers should register through official channels, keep their details accurate and check each scheme's rules separately before expecting a benefit.
