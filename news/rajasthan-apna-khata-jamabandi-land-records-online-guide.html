Rajasthan Apna Khata: How to Check Jamabandi and Land Records Online

A practical guide to Rajasthan's official Apna Khata portal, Jamabandi copies, mutation services and important limits of online land records.

Published November 2, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 Desk

Jaipur, November 2, 2026: Rajasthan residents can use the state government's Apna Khata portal to access digitised agricultural land-record information, including Jamabandi copies and mutation-related services. The portal is useful for an initial record check, but users should understand the difference between viewing information online and obtaining a certified document for an official or legal purpose.

What is Apna Khata?

Apna Khata is Rajasthan's official land-record portal. Its listed services include Jamabandi copies, mutation applications, consent-based partition applications, boundary-identification requests, mutation reports and related status information. The state's digital land-record ecosystem also includes e-Dharti and BhuNaksha services.

How to find a Jamabandi record

  1. Visit the official Apna Khata portal. Check that the address ends in rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Choose the relevant district and the subsequent revenue-area details requested by the portal.
  3. Enter the village and the available search details, such as account or landholder information, as prompted.
  4. Open the matching record and carefully compare the names, account details and land particulars.
  5. Save or print the information for personal reference, if the portal provides that option.

Can the online copy be used as a certified document?

Not in every situation. The Apna Khata portal itself warns that the information is provided for general information and that some mutations may not be updated immediately. It says the online copy should not be treated as a certified or authorised copy for use in a court or government office. For a certified copy, users are directed to apply through the prescribed kiosk or official process.

Other services available online

The portal lists online applications for mutation, consent partition and boundary identification, as well as application-status and mutation-report facilities. The broader Rajasthan land-record system connects textual records with mapping services such as BhuNaksha. Availability and workflow can vary by service and by the status of the underlying record.

What to do if the record looks incorrect

  • Do not assume that an online display automatically changes legal ownership.
  • Note the village, account number and details of the apparent discrepancy.
  • Use the official application or grievance route, or contact the relevant revenue office.
  • For a transaction, dispute or court matter, obtain the appropriate certified records and professional advice.

Protect yourself from fake land-record websites

Search results and social-media messages may lead to unofficial websites that imitate government services. Type the official portal address carefully, avoid paying unknown agents and never share OTPs or banking passwords to view a land record.

Conclusion

Apna Khata makes it easier to inspect Rajasthan's agricultural land-record information and access several revenue services online. It is a starting point for citizens, not a substitute for a certified record where one is legally required.

Understanding the main land-record terms

Jamabandi is a record of rights maintained by the revenue administration and can contain details of landholders, account numbers, land parcels and recorded rights. A khasra number identifies a particular parcel in the village record, while a khata or account groups holdings under a recorded account. Mutation refers to updating the revenue record after a change such as inheritance, sale, gift or another legally recognised event. These records are related, but they are not interchangeable with a registered sale deed or a final court determination of title.

How to read a record carefully

When a result appears, compare the district, tehsil, village, account number, khasra number, area and names. Check spelling variations and whether the record relates to the correct land parcel. If multiple people share a name, do not assume that the first search result is the right person. A mismatch should be checked with the local Patwari or revenue office before the information is used in a transaction, loan application or legal proceeding.

Mutation after inheritance or sale

A mutation entry records a change in the revenue record; it does not by itself create or extinguish ownership rights. The supporting documents and procedure depend on the reason for mutation. For inheritance, the office may require death and heirship-related documents; for a sale, the registered deed and relevant application details may be required. Applicants should check the current official checklist and keep the acknowledgement number to track the request.

Certified copies and legal use

A screen view or downloaded informational copy may not meet the requirements of a court, bank, government office or property transaction. The Apna Khata portal itself explains limitations around the online information and the timing of updates. If a certified copy is required, follow the official procedure for obtaining it from the competent revenue authority and confirm the seal, signature or digital verification method accepted by the receiving institution.

Common problems and how to respond

  • Name not found: check spelling, village selection and alternate account details before visiting the revenue office.
  • Old owner still appears: check whether mutation was filed, accepted and entered in the current record.
  • Area or boundary mismatch: compare the revenue record with the cadastral map and seek official demarcation where needed.
  • Portal unavailable: try again later or use the district's authorised service channel; do not use unofficial sites asking for sensitive data.
  • Disputed ownership: seek advice from a qualified legal professional and use the competent revenue or civil-court process.

Protect yourself from property fraud

Do not rely on a single online screenshot when buying land. Verify the registered deed, current revenue record, encumbrance information where applicable, possession, access and any pending dispute through the relevant official offices. Ask for original documents and obtain independent legal review before paying a substantial advance. Never share OTPs or banking credentials with an agent claiming to speed up a mutation.

Practical checklist before visiting an office

Carry the village and tehsil details, account or khasra number, identity documents and the reference number of any online application. Write down the exact correction or service required. Ask which office is responsible, what documents are missing and how to obtain a receipt. A written acknowledgement makes it easier to follow up and reduces the risk of repeated visits.
