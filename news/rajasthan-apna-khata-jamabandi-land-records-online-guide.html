Rajasthan Apna Khata: How to Check Jamabandi and Land Records Online

A practical guide to Rajasthan's official Apna Khata portal, Jamabandi copies, mutation services and important limits of online land records.

Published November 2, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 Desk

Jaipur, November 2, 2026: Rajasthan residents can use the state government's Apna Khata portal to access digitised agricultural land-record information, including Jamabandi copies and mutation-related services. The portal is useful for an initial record check, but users should understand the difference between viewing information online and obtaining a certified document for an official or legal purpose.

What is Apna Khata?

Apna Khata is Rajasthan's official land-record portal. Its listed services include Jamabandi copies, mutation applications, consent-based partition applications, boundary-identification requests, mutation reports and related status information. The state's digital land-record ecosystem also includes e-Dharti and BhuNaksha services.

How to find a Jamabandi record

  1. Visit the official Apna Khata portal. Check that the address ends in rajasthan.gov.in.
  2. Choose the relevant district and the subsequent revenue-area details requested by the portal.
  3. Enter the village and the available search details, such as account or landholder information, as prompted.
  4. Open the matching record and carefully compare the names, account details and land particulars.
  5. Save or print the information for personal reference, if the portal provides that option.

Can the online copy be used as a certified document?

Not in every situation. The Apna Khata portal itself warns that the information is provided for general information and that some mutations may not be updated immediately. It says the online copy should not be treated as a certified or authorised copy for use in a court or government office. For a certified copy, users are directed to apply through the prescribed kiosk or official process.

Other services available online

The portal lists online applications for mutation, consent partition and boundary identification, as well as application-status and mutation-report facilities. The broader Rajasthan land-record system connects textual records with mapping services such as BhuNaksha. Availability and workflow can vary by service and by the status of the underlying record.

What to do if the record looks incorrect

  • Do not assume that an online display automatically changes legal ownership.
  • Note the village, account number and details of the apparent discrepancy.
  • Use the official application or grievance route, or contact the relevant revenue office.
  • For a transaction, dispute or court matter, obtain the appropriate certified records and professional advice.

Protect yourself from fake land-record websites

Search results and social-media messages may lead to unofficial websites that imitate government services. Type the official portal address carefully, avoid paying unknown agents and never share OTPs or banking passwords to view a land record.

Conclusion

Apna Khata makes it easier to inspect Rajasthan's agricultural land-record information and access several revenue services online. It is a starting point for citizens, not a substitute for a certified record where one is legally required.
