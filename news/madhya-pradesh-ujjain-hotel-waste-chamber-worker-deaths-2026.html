Four Workers Die During Cleaning of Waste Chamber at Ujjain Hotel

Four workers died after entering a waste chamber at a hotel in Ujjain. Police are investigating the circumstances and whether toxic gas or oxygen deficiency caused the incident.

Published November 1, 2026 · Updated November 2, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 Desk

Ujjain, November 1, 2026: Four workers died after an incident involving a waste chamber at a hotel on Dewas Road in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh. Initial reports raised the possibility of toxic gas exposure or suffocation. Police and local authorities are investigating the circumstances.

Reports by The Indian Express and Hindustan Times said the workers entered a chamber used to collect food waste and other material. Two other people were reportedly taken for medical treatment after attempting to help. The exact sequence of events and the cause of death remain subject to investigation.

What are investigators examining?

Authorities are expected to examine the atmosphere inside the chamber, ventilation, safety equipment, the work procedure and whether the workers were given appropriate training and supervision. The cause should not be treated as confirmed until the investigation and any relevant medical or forensic findings are complete.

Compensation reported

The Indian Express reported that the Madhya Pradesh government announced financial assistance of ₹4 lakh for the family of each worker who died. Families should seek confirmation from local administration about the official order and disbursement process.

Why confined-space work is dangerous

Waste chambers, tanks and other enclosed spaces can contain low oxygen levels or dangerous gases. Entry should only take place after proper atmospheric testing, ventilation, trained supervision and suitable protective equipment are in place. Untrained rescue attempts can put additional lives at risk.
