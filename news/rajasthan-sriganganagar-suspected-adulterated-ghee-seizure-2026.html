22,138 Litres of Suspected Adulterated Ghee Seized in Rajasthan's Sri Ganganagar; Three Arrested

Authorities seized 22,138 litres of suspected adulterated ghee during raids at two business premises in Sri Ganganagar. Food samples have been sent for laboratory testing.

Published November 1, 2026 · Updated November 2, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 Desk

Sri Ganganagar, November 1, 2026: Authorities seized 22,138 litres of suspected adulterated ghee during action at two business premises in Rajasthan's Sri Ganganagar district. Three people were arrested, according to media reports citing police and food-safety officials.

The reported seizure included 16,394 litres from one premises and another 5,744 litres from a business location near the Suratgarh bypass. The estimated value of the seized stock was reported at around ₹1.60 crore. Officials also reportedly recovered packaging material and other products for examination.

Samples sent for laboratory testing

Food-safety officials have sent samples for laboratory analysis. Investigators are examining allegations that expired or returned ghee may have been repackaged and offered for sale. These are allegations under investigation; the seized stock should not be described as proven adulterated until laboratory findings and the legal process establish the facts.

What consumers should check

Buyers should check the seal, manufacturing and best-before dates, FSSAI licence details and purchase receipt when buying packaged food. If a product appears unusual or has an abnormal smell, consumers should stop using it and contact the relevant food-safety authority.

What happens next?

Further action will depend on laboratory results and the police investigation. Consumers should avoid circulating unverified claims about brands or sellers while the inquiry is ongoing.
