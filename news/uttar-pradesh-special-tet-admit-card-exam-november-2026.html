UP Special TET 2026 Admit Card Released; Examination Scheduled for November 3

The Uttar Pradesh Special Teacher Eligibility Test admit card has been released. The examination is scheduled for November 3 at six divisional headquarters.

Published November 1, 2026 · Updated November 2, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 Desk

Lucknow, November 1, 2026: The admit card for the Uttar Pradesh Special Teacher Eligibility Test (UP Special TET) 2026 has been released, according to reports. The examination is scheduled for November 3 at six divisional headquarters in the state.

Candidates can access the hall ticket through the official Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission portal. The reported login process requires the candidate's OTR ID and password. Candidates should use the official website rather than links shared by unknown social-media accounts.

Check these details on the admit card

  • Candidate name and photograph
  • Roll number and application details
  • Examination date and reporting time
  • Examination centre name and full address
  • Instructions and permitted documents

What should candidates do before the exam?

Download and review the admit card as soon as possible. If the name, photograph, centre or other information appears incorrect, contact the Commission through its official channels before the examination. Plan travel to reach the centre on time and follow the instructions printed on the hall ticket.

Official website

Check the UPESSC official website for the admit card and any updates to the examination schedule. The Commission's notices should be treated as the final source for centre and timing information.

What candidates should verify on the admit card

After downloading the admit card, candidates should check their name, roll number, registration details, photograph, signature, examination date, reporting time and centre address. If any detail appears incorrect, contact the examination authority through the official helpdesk immediately. Do not wait until the day of the examination to report a spelling error or a mismatch in the photograph. Keep a copy of the submitted complaint or acknowledgement.

Documents to carry to the examination centre

Candidates should follow the instructions printed on the admit card and the latest official examination notice. Usually, the required items include a printed admit card and an accepted original photo identity document. Additional photographs or declarations may be required if the notice says so. Do not assume that a digital copy on a mobile phone will be accepted in place of a printed document.

Exam-day planning

  • Locate the centre in advance and estimate travel time, including traffic and public-transport delays.
  • Read the reporting time and gate-closing instructions carefully.
  • Carry only the permitted stationery and documents.
  • Follow the instructions on prohibited electronic devices, watches, bags and study material.
  • Listen to invigilators and complete identity verification before entering the examination hall.

How to prepare in the final hours

At this stage, candidates should prioritise revision of familiar topics, key definitions and practice questions rather than attempting to learn an entire syllabus overnight. Review the official exam pattern and instructions. Sleep, hydration and a planned journey can be as important as last-minute revision. Candidates should avoid relying on leaked-paper claims or unofficial answer keys circulating on social media.

What if the admit card cannot be downloaded?

Check the official portal's notice section and try again using the registration details exactly as submitted. If the problem continues, use the official helpline or contact channel listed by the examination authority. Keep screenshots of the error and the registration acknowledgement. Never pay an agent who promises to generate an admit card or change a centre through an unofficial link.

After the examination

Keep the admit card and any attendance or examination record until the recruitment or eligibility process is complete. Follow official notices for answer keys, objection windows, results and document verification. Deadlines can be strict, and an objection usually requires a prescribed fee or evidence where the authority's rules provide for it. The examination body's website is the final source for dates and procedural instructions.
