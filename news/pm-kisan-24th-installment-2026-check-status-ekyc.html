PM-KISAN 24th Installment 2026: How Farmers Can Check Payment Status and Complete eKYC

The government says the 24th PM-KISAN instalment is scheduled for 30 October 2026. Learn how to check status, complete eKYC and understand verification holds.

Published November 2, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 Desk

New Delhi, November 2, 2026: The official PM-KISAN portal says the 24th instalment of the Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi scheme is scheduled for release on October 30, 2026, in Banaskantha, Gujarat. Farmers should use the official portal or mobile application to check their individual payment status rather than relying on forwarded messages or unofficial websites.

What is PM-KISAN?

PM-KISAN is a central government income-support scheme for eligible landholding farmer families. The National Portal of India describes the benefit as ₹6,000 per year, paid in three equal instalments of ₹2,000, subject to scheme rules and exclusions. A scheduled national release does not mean every registered farmer will necessarily receive a payment on the same day: eligibility, bank validation and verification can affect individual records.

How to check your PM-KISAN status

  1. Open the official PM-KISAN website.
  2. Find the farmer services or “Know Your Status” facility.
  3. Enter the requested registration or identity details on the official page.
  4. Review the displayed status, including eligibility, eKYC and payment information.
  5. If the portal shows a discrepancy, contact the designated agriculture office or an authorised Common Service Centre (CSC).

Why eKYC matters

The PM-KISAN homepage states that eKYC is mandatory for registered farmers. It also says biometric-based eKYC is available through CSC VLE logins, the PM-KISAN mobile application and authorised state-user logins. Farmers should not share OTPs, Aadhaar details or bank credentials with callers claiming they can release an instalment.

Why a payment may be held

The official portal currently flags suspected cases that may fall under scheme exclusion rules. Examples listed include land ownership acquired after February 1, 2019, and cases where more than one family member appears to be receiving benefits. The portal says benefits in suspected cases may be temporarily withheld while physical verification is completed. Farmers should check their own “Know Your Status” record and follow the official verification process rather than assuming that a delay automatically means permanent rejection.

Important precautions

  • Use only the government PM-KISAN portal or authorised service channels.
  • Check that the bank account and Aadhaar-related details in the official record are correct.
  • Do not pay an agent who guarantees approval or immediate release.
  • Keep application references and acknowledgements for follow-up.

Bottom line

The 24th instalment date is listed on the official portal, but each farmer's entitlement depends on eligibility and successful verification. The safest next step is to check the official status page and resolve any eKYC or record issue through authorised channels.
