AP Voter Poll: Cost of Living Was a Major Factor for Nearly 9 in 10 Midterm Voters

Published November 6, 2026 · 2:33 p.m. ET · Rajasthan News 24 US Desk

The Associated Press Voter Poll found that nearly nine in ten 2026 midterm voters considered the cost of living a major or crucial factor in their vote, highlighting affordability as a central election issue.

WASHINGTON, November 6, 2026: The Associated Press Voter Poll found that nearly nine in ten 2026 midterm voters considered the cost of living a major or crucial factor in their vote, highlighting affordability as a central election issue.

What voters said mattered

About six in ten voters who said the cost of living was a major or crucial factor supported Democratic candidates for the U.S. House, AP reported. Roughly two-thirds of respondents said President Donald Trump's policies contributed to inflation, while around seven in ten said the economy favored wealthy people.

The survey also found broad skepticism about artificial intelligence: about six in ten voters said AI would harm society. Many respondents said they believed administration policies had worsened health-care costs. Those are respondents' views, not independent measurements of the economic effect of any policy.

How AP conducted the voter poll

AP says the 2026 effort combined a pre-election survey of about 75,000 people across all 50 states with roughly 25,000 Election Day interviews at polling places in 15 states. Respondents were verified voters, and the poll was designed to explain who voted and which issues mattered to them. It is not an official vote count and does not replace state certification.

Why the distinction matters

Election results establish who won. A voter survey helps describe how voters say they made their choices. The poll offers a large-scale view of affordability and other concerns, but reported attitudes should not be treated as proof that any single policy or event determined the national result.
