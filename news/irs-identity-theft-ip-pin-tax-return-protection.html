IRS Identity Theft: How an IP PIN Can Help Protect Your Tax Return

IRS Identity Theft: How an IP PIN Can Help Protect Your Tax Return. A practical US guide to key rules, steps, common pitfalls and official resources for American readers.

Published November 6, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 US Desk · Educational guide

What Americans should know

Tax-related identity theft can occur when someone uses another person's information to file a fraudulent tax return or claim a refund. The IRS offers an Identity Protection Personal Identification Number (IP PIN) to eligible individuals as an added identity check on federal returns.

How the rules and choices work

An IP PIN is a six-digit number that changes periodically and must be entered correctly when required. It should be protected like a password. The IRS has separate procedures for people who discover a return was filed using their information.

Practical steps to take

If you suspect tax identity theft, follow IRS identity-theft instructions, respond to genuine IRS notices and consider reporting broader identity theft at IdentityTheft.gov. Secure email and financial accounts, enable multifactor authentication and monitor credit reports.

Common mistakes and warning signs

An IP PIN does not prevent every kind of identity theft. Never share it with an unsolicited caller or a person promising to release a refund.

Frequently asked questions

Are these rules the same in every state?

Not always. Federal programmes have nationwide rules, but states may administer benefits differently and add protections, deadlines or eligibility conditions. Check the official agency for the state where you live or work.

Can I rely on an online estimate?

Use calculators and general guides for planning, not as official approval. Confirm important figures with the responsible agency, lender, insurer, plan administrator or tax professional.

What records should I keep?

Save applications, statements, contracts, notices, receipts, confirmation numbers and correspondence. A clear timeline makes it easier to correct errors or request a review.
