India's October 2026 GST Collection Rises 22.9% to ₹2.31 Lakh Crore

India's gross GST revenue reached ₹2,31,290 crore in October 2026, up 22.9% year on year. Import-related collections recorded particularly strong growth.

Published November 1, 2026 · Updated November 2, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 Desk

New Delhi, November 1, 2026: India's gross Goods and Services Tax (GST) collection rose 22.9% year on year to ₹2,31,290 crore in October 2026, according to figures reported by the Finance Ministry and covered by Reuters and other news organisations.

Net GST revenue after refunds was reported at ₹2,03,356 crore, an increase of 25.6% compared with the same month last year. Import-related revenue rose 42.2% to ₹72,246 crore, while domestic revenue increased 15.8%.

What do the October figures show?

The headline collection reflects tax paid on domestic transactions as well as imports. The faster rise in import-related receipts contributed to the overall increase. Net collections also depend on refunds, so gross and net figures should not be compared as if they were the same measure.

April to October collections

Gross GST collections during the first seven months of the 2026-27 financial year were reported at approximately ₹14.78 lakh crore, up 13.2% from the corresponding period a year earlier. The figures provide an indication of tax receipts and formal economic activity, but do not by themselves measure household welfare or growth across every sector.

Why GST data matters

GST collections are watched by governments, businesses and analysts because they provide a recurring snapshot of taxable economic transactions. Monthly comparisons can be influenced by the timing of payments, refunds, imports and changes in the tax base. A single month's increase should therefore be read alongside broader economic indicators.
