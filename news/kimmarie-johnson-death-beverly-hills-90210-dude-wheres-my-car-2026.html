Kimmarie Johnson Dies at 50: ‘Beverly Hills, 90210’ and ‘Dude, Where’s My Car?’ Actress Remembered

Kimmarie Johnson, the actress and model who appeared in Beverly Hills, 90210 and Dude, Where’s My Car?, has died at 50. Here is what her family and reports have confirmed.

Published November 6, 2026 · Rajasthan News 24 US Desk

Kimmarie Johnson, an actress, model and former beauty pageant titleholder known for appearances in Beverly Hills, 90210 and the 2000 comedy Dude, Where’s My Car?, has died at age 50. Her niece, Rhonda Scott, confirmed the death in a statement to ABC News. Johnson died on November 4, 2026, after a medical emergency at her Los Angeles home, according to her family.

What has been confirmed about her death?

Scott told ABC News that Johnson experienced a medical emergency at home on October 27 and was taken to Cedars-Sinai Medical Center in Los Angeles. Scott described doctors’ diagnosis as a “heart aneurysm condition.” That wording is attributed to the family member and should not be treated as an independently released medical report or a formal determination of cause of death.

As of this report, no public medical examiner statement establishing an official cause of death was identified in the cited reporting. It is therefore more accurate to say that her family reported a medical emergency and a heart-related diagnosis than to state that a specific condition has been officially certified as the cause.

Her television and film appearances

Johnson appeared as Judy in an episode of Beverly Hills, 90210 and had a small role in Dude, Where’s My Car?, the 2000 comedy starring Ashton Kutcher and Seann William Scott. Her credits also included television and independent film projects, including V.I.P. and other productions.

Those credits explain why her name has resurfaced in searches connected to the long-running teen drama and the early-2000s comedy. Search interest can rise quickly when viewers revisit an actor’s best-known screen appearances after news of their death.

From pageants and modeling to acting

Johnson was also known for modeling and beauty pageants. Entertainment reports say she competed in the Miss Pennsylvania USA pageant as a teenager and later moved to Los Angeles to pursue work in entertainment. Her career extended beyond acting into modeling, producing and beauty-related business projects.

What her family said

In a statement shared with ABC News, her niece remembered Johnson as someone whose positive energy affected people around her. The family said they would miss her and continue to honor her memory.
