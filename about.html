About Rajasthan News 24

Rajasthan News 24 is an English-language digital news publication covering important developments in India, the United States and Rajasthan, including local news from Bhilwara.

Our purpose

Our aim is to make important developments easier to understand through clear, original summaries, relevant context and transparent source attribution. We cover public affairs, politics, business, education, jobs, technology and local developments.

How we prepare articles

Articles are researched using available reporting, official information and primary sources where possible. We write in our own words, distinguish allegations from established facts, and avoid presenting projections or unverified claims as confirmed outcomes. AI tools may assist research or editing, but editorial responsibility for published material remains with the publication.

Who writes our articles?

Rajasthan News 24 Desk is the collective editorial byline used for articles prepared for this publication. It does not represent a fabricated individual reporter or imply credentials that have not been verified.

Corrections and accountability

We review credible factual concerns and make material corrections when evidence supports them. Read our Editorial Policy and Corrections Policy.
